Air Quality Analysis & Prediction

Analyzing pollution and transportation data to forecast AQI values with machine learning

The Problem

🏭

Urban Pollution Crisis

Rapid urbanization and industrial growth have led to significant deterioration in air quality, affecting millions of people's health and quality of life.

🚗

Transportation Impact

Vehicle emissions contribute significantly to air pollution. Understanding the relationship between traffic and air quality is crucial for urban planning.

📊

Need for Prediction

Accurate AQI predictions enable cities to implement proactive measures, alert citizens, and plan environmental interventions effectively.

🔍

Data-Driven Solutions

By analyzing historical pollution and traffic data, we can identify patterns and predict future AQI values to support smart city initiatives.

Outlier Detection Methods

🔍 IQR Method

Technique: Interquartile Range statistical method

Formula: Bounds = Q1 ± 1.5 × IQR

Pollutants Checked: PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3

Why It Matters: Identifies extreme pollution events that may require immediate action.

🌲 Isolation Forest

Technique: Machine learning-based anomaly detection

Algorithm: Contamination rate = 0.05

Multivariate: Considers all features simultaneously

Why It Matters: Detects complex patterns that single-feature analysis might miss.

Conclusion

Air quality prediction is crucial for modern urban environments. This project demonstrates a comprehensive data analytics approach to understanding pollution patterns and forecasting Air Quality Index values.

Key Takeaways:

  • 🎯 Particulate Matter Dominates: PM2.5 and PM10 are the strongest predictors of AQI, emphasizing the need for emission controls.
  • 📍 City-Specific Variations: Different cities exhibit distinct pollution profiles, requiring tailored interventions.
  • 🚗 Transportation Connection: Traffic volume shows measurable correlation with air quality, supporting eco-friendly transportation policies.
  • 🤖 Predictive Power: Machine learning models can accurately forecast AQI, enabling proactive alert systems.
  • 💡 Data-Driven Policy: Evidence-based insights support effective environmental planning and public health decisions.
